      ✦About Catalyst for Change

      Catalyst for Change is the annual design competition of Justice In Residence, founded by Marlene Paez Dukes. It pairs emerging creatives with movement organizations and creative agencies to work on issues communities are living with now. Every 2026 participant was a current student. Its line: creativity where change happens.

      The 2026 briefs came from Equity and Transformation (EAT), Men Making a Difference (M.M.A.D.), Territory and the Chicago City Council Committee on Environmental Protection and Energy. Partners included the Design Museum of Chicago, ALT Space Chicago and Mutuall Design. Johnnie R. Lovett, Managing Partner at Unsurfaced™, served on the judging panel.
