      ✦About the conversation

      The Gray Matter Experience inspires, educates, and empowers the next generation of Black changemakers by bridging the entrepreneurial knowledge gap. Founder Britney Robbins hosted the session with the Gray Matter cohort at Gwendolyn Brooks College Prep, in conversation with Johnnie R. Lovett, Managing Partner at Unsurfaced™, on how a career gets built and how it turns into something you own.

      For Lovett it was a return. He grew up in Roseland and first met entrepreneurship in a class inside this same building.
