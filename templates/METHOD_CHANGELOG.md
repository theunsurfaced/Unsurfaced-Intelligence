# The Method, as it changed

The record is only honest if the rules that produced it are dated too. Every change to the Cultural Read Method, the writer's laws or the grading is listed here with its day. The Method itself lives in `templates/CULTURAL_READ_METHOD.md`; the worker carries an exact copy and the ritual gate fails when the two drift.

| Day | Version | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-03 | Method 4.0 | The Method is loaded word for word as the writer's standing instruction; the ritual gate keeps the worker's copy in sync. The time law (every evidence line dated and banded) and the number law (every figure in an evidence line the read cites) govern every read. |
| 2026-10-06 | Method 4.1 | The house style: voices credited to people by generation or role and kept across time; three years of attention for a RECON; the record across years; the numbers after the findings; the copy desk's style checks; the depth law. |
| 2026-10-06 | RECON deep | Four passes (analyst, strategist, writer, editor) on the same evidence; the editor's pass lands as the compile; the copy desk after. |
| 2026-10-08 | Method 4.2 | The editing laws: say it once; confidence frames the claim; limits first; every section earns its page; two readers, one document (the client cut). |
| 2026-10-08 | EXCAVATE writer | The headline law on the live writer: line one is 6 to 12 words, present tense, one claim about people, no figure, source, year or list; line two carries the one figure that proves it. The thin-ground law: under twelve lines the read says it is building and stays small. A quick read writes without thinking under a 45 second deadline. |
| 2026-10-08 | The record | Every door read carries one measurable watch (a measure, an operator, a value, a date) graded on the database's own counts when its date comes; a miss stays. The blind-spots line on every read. The earned law replaces the cadence: a reading is written when a subject is new or moved past the threshold, at most six a night, as few as none. |
| 2026-10-08 | The bench | Twenty frozen packs written and graded on the laws (the code's checks and one reader rubric) on every writer change; the gate refuses a writer the bench has not seen or that fell below the floor of 70. |
