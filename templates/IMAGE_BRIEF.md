# The image brief, house style v2 (humanity first)

Version house-v2.1. Loaded word for word as the standing instruction for every image the house makes. Edit it here; the worker carries an exact copy and the ritual gate fails if the two drift. Everything here except the two lines marked HARD is a default the house's picks revise.

## What a house image is for

An image on a cover, a window or a hero tells the story of the finding it sits over. A reader who sees it without the headline should be able to guess the headline. It is a moment with a person in it, specific and unposed, carrying one idea through one detail.

## The brief

Fill these before anything is made:

1. PERSON: an invented person, specific and imperfect, of any age, body, place or background. Never a real or famous person (HARD).
2. MOMENT: what they are doing, in the middle of doing it.
3. PLACE: where, with the texture of a real place.
4. LIGHT: the light, named (sodium platform light, blue hour, hard midday sun, a workshop window).
5. LENS: 35mm film, candid, natural grain, unless the finding asks for something else.
6. DETAIL: the one detail that carries the idea. The headphones. The leash wound twice. The umbrella in the sun.
7. RED: where the single deep signal red sits.
8. STORY: "a reader who sees this without the headline should think ___." If this line cannot be written, the slot is typographic and nothing is made.

## Defaults the house learns from

- Lean away from text or lettering in the image; typography is the page's job.
- A brand's world can be named for context (a sneaker store, an archive room) without its marks drawn. No logos, trademarks or protected product designs rendered (HARD).
- Unbranded clothing, plain surfaces, real everyday people, varied ages and backgrounds.
- Palette restraint: the house red appears in one detail of the scene, never as a wash over it.
- Wit is welcome when the detail is true to the finding.
- Composition with one clear subject and calm room in the lower third for a headline.

## The prompt the model receives

Editorial photograph for the cover of a weekly culture magazine. {scene} {light} Shot on 35mm film with natural grain, candid and unposed. Real everyday people with varied ages and backgrounds, unbranded clothing and plain, unlettered surfaces throughout. A strong, simple composition with one clear subject and calm, uncluttered space in the lower third of the frame for a headline. One small detail in deep signal red: {red}. No lettering, signage or logos anywhere in the frame.
